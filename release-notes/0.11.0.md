## What's new

- **Formatted numbers and unit safety.** EDA and chart tools parse numbers with units, currencies and negative notations (`12%`, `3억원`, `△5`, `(1,234)`), and refuse columns or comparisons that mix units.
- **Markdown tables as data sources.** `eda_profile`, `eda_analyze`, `build_chart` and `suggest_chart` can read pipe tables directly from vault `.md` notes with a 1-based `table` parameter, and flag summary-row candidates.
- **Period and plan comparisons.** `eda_analyze` adds `compare` for two-period comparisons and value-against-plan comparisons with common-group totals.
- **Report-ready charts and provenance.** `build_chart` adds `report`, `print` and `slides` styles, `target` lines, `highlight` selectors, `unit`, `caption`, `source.analysis`, `title_from`, `# chart-hands source:` provenance comments, and deterministic `facts`, `titleDraft`, `factLines` and `reportSnippet` outputs.
- **Chart suggestions and presentation advice.** New `suggest_chart` tool recommends chart types and runnable `build_chart` arguments; `check_chart` and `build_chart` report non-blocking presentation advice; `read_charts` reports provenance and advice counts; `get_chart_guide` adds the `report` topic.
- **Provider reapproval.** Data tools raise their result limit to 16,000 characters. The tool descriptor changes (new suggest_chart tool, new arguments and result limits), so hosts must reapprove the provider.
