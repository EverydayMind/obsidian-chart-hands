## What's new

- **Built-in Plotly schema check and fence-tolerant `check_chart`.** `build_chart` now loads Plotly and runs full `Plotly.validate` checks directly when Plotly is available, and `check_chart` strips surrounding ` ```plotly ` or ` ~~~plotly ` code fences automatically.
- **Direct charting from EDA results.** `eda_analyze` aggregate and compare results now include ready-to-run `chartArgs` for `build_chart` (retained even when `display` or `chart` arrays are trimmed for size), and `build_chart` marks `staticBlock` outputs as HWPX/PPTX export only (`staticBlockUse`).
- **Fewer avoidable tool errors.** CSV paths now accept `table: 1` without error, combining `title` with `title_from` keeps `title` and lists `title_from` in `ignoredArgs`, empty `filter` arrays are omitted from provenance comments and automatic captions, and `source.analysis` key conflicts list the actual offending keys with a corrected example.
- **Cleaner fact titles and multi-series label highlighting.** Fact scopes are joined with ` · ` instead of parentheses, trend title drafts drop inner parentheses, `highlight.labels` gets its own `Highlighted:` / `강조:` title template and now works across multi-series `bar`, `barh` and `combo` charts (including static blocks).
- **Better chart suggestions.** `suggest_chart` reports same-unit `referenceCandidates` for `target` goals without a reference or target value, and treats two explicit periods paired with prior-period comparison wording (`전년 대비`, `전년 동기 대비`, `작년 대비`, `전월 대비`, `전기 대비`) as chronological.
- **Provider reapproval.** The tool descriptor changes (build_chart and check_chart wording, provider instructions), so hosts must reapprove the provider.
