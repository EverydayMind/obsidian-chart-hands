## What's new

- **English request parsing in `suggest_chart`.** English requests now support negation (`except`, `without`, `not`, `n't`, …), period direction phrasing (`compared with/to`, `against`, `relative to`, `from A to B`, `as the baseline`, `year over year`/`YoY`), highlight and rank-only wording (`only the top 5`, `three largest declines`, `as a table`), target values (`target of 100`, `90% target`), and expanded goal keywords.
- **English units and currency codes.** `eda_profile`, `eda_analyze`, `build_chart` and `suggest_chart` now parse suffix and prefix English units (`K`, `M`, `B`, `bn`, `mn`, `thousand`, `million`, `billion`, `USD`, `EUR`, `KRW`, `JPY`, `GBP`, `pp`, `pcs`, `units`, `people`, `cases`, `items`, including parenthesized negatives, and currency symbols with a scale such as `$120K` or `$3 million`) and format multi-letter English units with a consistent space (`3 million`, `1,200 USD`, `12 pp`).
- **Month-name and half-year periods.** Period parsing and column classification now recognize English month names (`Jan 2025`, `January 2025`, `Jan-2025`, `2025 Jan`) and half-years (`H1 2025`, `2025-H1`, `H1`, `H2`), while ignoring standalone words like `may` in free-text requests.
- **Side-by-side period comparisons for `compare`.** When `goal` is `compare` on data with repeated categories and a period column, `suggest_chart` leads with a side-by-side period comparison bar chart when two periods are applied, or cautions that the aggregate combines periods otherwise.
- **README: Better with Verbex.** A new section shows plain-language report requests, a real tool output with its provenance line and fact sentences, and why the tools (not the model) compute every number.
