## What's new

- **Readable highlights across several series.** When `highlight.labels` picks categories in a multi-series bar chart, each series keeps its own colour on those categories and a pale tint of it on the rest, so the series stay distinguishable (print style keeps its patterns).
- **Fewer failed highlight calls.** `highlight.labels` given beside `top`, `bottom`, `below_target` or `above_target` is used as is, and the other selector is listed in `ignoredArgs`. Two rank selectors fail with the selector names that were received.
- **Clearer highlight errors on line and other non-bar charts.** The error names the chart type and says to change it to `bar` or `barh`, or omit `highlight`.
